# Vocabulary Builder (Словарный запас) Privacy Policy
Effective date: 2026-11-20
Developer: Lali Orudjova ("we"). Contact: support form https://forms.gle/VchbnfENUyMqGE2P7. App ID: com.lalishka.vocab.

## 1. Summary
Vocabulary Builder works offline. It has no accounts, no servers of our own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The dictionary, your test results and your learning progress are stored only on your device. We do not receive, sell or share them. The only outside service the app uses is RevenueCat, which handles in-app purchases (see section 3).

## 2. Data processed on your device
- **Learning data**: the language you chose, placement test answers and results, your words and review schedule, daily goal, streaks and settings. Your individual test answers never leave the device.
- **Notifications**: optional reminders. These are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push service is used and no data leaves the device. You can turn them off in the app or in system settings.
- **Widgets and Lock Screen**: the word-of-the-day widget and Lock Screen activities read data from the app on your device.
- **Export and import**: you can export your progress as a JSON file and import it again. The file is not encrypted and contains your word list. You decide where to save or send it.
- **Erase data**: Settings → Erase data deletes your progress, test history and settings for both languages on this device.
- **System backups**: if device backup is on (iCloud or Google), your operating system may include the app's data in your backup. Apple or Google manage those backups under their own policies.

## 3. Third parties
- **RevenueCat (in-app purchases).** To sell Vocabulary Pro, restore purchases and check whether Pro is active, the app uses the RevenueCat SDK (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). RevenueCat acts as our data processor. When the app loads Pro offers, makes a purchase, restores purchases or checks Pro status, RevenueCat receives:
  - a random anonymous App User ID created by the SDK. It is not linked to your name, e-mail or any account;
  - purchase history: products, transaction dates, the Apple receipt or Google purchase token, and subscription status;
  - technical data: device type, operating system, app version, locale and currency code, and the time the app was last seen.

  Like any network request, this also exposes your IP address to RevenueCat. RevenueCat does not receive your words, test answers or progress. It does not collect payment card details; Apple and Google handle payment. We do not use RevenueCat for advertising or cross-app tracking. RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
- **App stores** (Apple, Google) process downloads, payments, subscriptions and crash reports under their own policies.
- No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not request advertising identifiers (IDFA or AAID).

## 4. Retention and deletion
- Data on your device stays until you erase it in the app (Settings → Erase data) or uninstall the app.
- Purchase records at RevenueCat are kept while they are needed to provide and restore your purchases. To ask us to delete your RevenueCat record, use the support form https://forms.gle/VchbnfENUyMqGE2P7. Deleting it does not cancel a subscription. Cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
- Apple and Google keep their own purchase records.

## 5. Children
Vocabulary Builder is made for teens and adults. It is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA/UK where required). We do not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child provided data to us, contact us through https://forms.gle/VchbnfENUyMqGE2P7 and we will help delete it.

## 6. Your rights
- **GDPR / UK GDPR:** learning data is processed on your device under your control. Purchase data is processed through RevenueCat to provide the purchase you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)) and to prevent fraud (Art. 6(1)(f)). You may ask for access to or deletion of your data through https://forms.gle/VchbnfENUyMqGE2P7, and you may complain to your supervisory authority.
- **CCPA/CPRA (California):** we do not sell or share personal information and do not use sensitive personal information.

## 8. Security
Your data is protected by your device's operating-system sandbox. Use a device lock. Data sent to RevenueCat is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

## 9. Changes
We will update this page and the effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app or store listing.
